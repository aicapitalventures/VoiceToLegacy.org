Accessibility

Accessibility Commitment

Voice to Legacy™ seeks to make the prospective-author journey understandable and usable across devices and abilities.

Available alternatives

Prospective authors may choose a fillable PDF, phone conversation, Zoom meeting, email, or text message. A person who cannot use one method may request another.

Website design

The starter site uses semantic structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, descriptive alternative text, and high-contrast presentation.

Request assistance

Call or text 502-270-8828 or email accounting@divinityxenterprises.com to request communication or document assistance.
