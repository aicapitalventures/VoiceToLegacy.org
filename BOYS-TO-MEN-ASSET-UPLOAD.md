# Boys to Men Cover Asset Upload Gate

Before this branch is merged, upload the approved cover images to `assets/img/` using these exact filenames:

- `boys-to-men-front.webp`
- `boys-to-men-back.webp`

The Published Work page already references these paths. Both images must be present before merge to prevent broken cover images on the live site.

## Approved source files

- Front cover source: `frnt.jpg`
- Back cover source: `bck(1).jpg`

`assets: add Boys to Men front and back covers`

## Validation

- Confirm the front cover appears first and larger.
- Confirm the back cover appears second.
- Confirm both images remain readable on desktop and mobile.
